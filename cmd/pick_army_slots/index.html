How to calibrate:
1. Click Open Dropdown on Game to expand the Saved Recipes popup on BlueStacks.
2. Click a Slot card on the right (e.g. Slot 2 - Army 2).
3. Click on the image where that army card is located.
4. Click Test Tap to watch the game immediately select that army!
5. Click Save Boxes when done.
