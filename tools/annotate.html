How to use

Drop p0.png on this page (or use Open image…).

Then trace the red deploy line: pick Points and click along it, or pick Freehand and drag along it. Zoom with the wheel; Pan to move.

When done, hit Copy points and paste the result back into chat.

Points (image pixels)

Tips

Start anywhere on the line and go around it once. Corners matter more than straight parts — a click at each sharp turn is enough.
